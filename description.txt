If you're tired of vanilla spears but you want a conservative, "vanilla+" style mod that improves them, this might just be the mod you're looking for!

Nice & Balanced Spear Buffs makes all spears a lot more comfortable and easy to use with a brand new thrust animation that allows you to quickly retarget the spear!

Additionally, this mod also buffs a handful of spears, giving them new effects.

List of changes:
-All spears have autoswing.
-All spears except the Ghastly Glaive have a new thrusting animation.
-The Rotted Fork inflicts Bleeding debuff.
-Dark Lance inflicts Shadowflame debuff like in 1.4.4.
-Mushroom Spear and its projectile inflict Confused debuff.
-Mushroom Spear's Mushroom projectiles have been adjusted to better reflect the new animation.
-Mushroom Spear's Mushroom projectiles now deal 100% of the base damage, up from 33%.
-Chlorophyte Partisan and its projectile inflict Venom debuff.
-Spore Cloud (Chlorophyte Partisan's projectile) grows bigger in size over time.
-North Pole and its projectiles inflict Frostbite debuff.
-North Pole has new particle effects.

Note: The Rotted Fork, Dark Lance and Gungnir will receive their 1.4.4 projectiles when tModLoader updates to that version.
