Shiny's Remix is a "rebalance" mod that changes (mostly) a bunch of vanilla weapons, focusing on making them more fun and unique.

The core aspects of the mod are:

[list]
    [*]Vanilla+. Nothing that looks out of place in regular Terraria.
    [*]Modularity. Mod is divided into several parts, you can toggle each one individually.
    [*]Minimalism. Does not add new content; instead refreshes existing content.
    [*]Compatibility with other vanilla+ mods: Thorium, Storm's Additions, TRAE, Consolaria.
[/list]
